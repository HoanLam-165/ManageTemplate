# Template Workspace — Document Map

## 1. Product Plan
`KẾ HOẠCH PHÁT TRIỂN SẢN PHẨM — TEMPLATE WORKSPACE v2.md`

Answers:
- What are we building?
- Why are we building it?
- Who is it for?
- What is MVP?
- What is intentionally out of scope?
- What hypotheses must be validated?

## 2. Implementation Skill
`template-workspace-skill/`

Answers:
- How should the CLI implement it?
- What are the locked technical decisions?
- What phase are we in?
- What tests must pass?
- When must the AI stop and ask?

## 3. Idea / Research Notes
`Ý tưởng phát triển/`

Contains:
- brainstorming;
- rejected ideas;
- future possibilities;
- thesis thoughts;
- unresolved questions.

## Recommended workflow

1. Discuss product idea in `Ý tưởng phát triển`.
2. When a product decision is accepted, update Product Plan.
3. When a technical decision is accepted, update `00-project-rules.md`.
4. CLI works only from `template-workspace-skill`.
5. Human reviews every phase gate.
